# Questions for you (product / Ranked experience)

Answer these yourself to the best of your ability. Keep answers short and concrete.  
This is **separate** from the deep-research prompt — broad strategy will come from the researcher; these lock **product rules** and your local Ranked reality.

If you don’t know, write `unknown` — better than guessing.

## A. Lobby & coin flip

1. When you queue Ranked trio, who sees the coin flip result first in-game, and how do you usually communicate it into the tool?
2. Is coin flip always “winner first picks,” or are there seasons/modes where that differs?
3. Do you ever want to **override** coin flip in the tool (practice / scrims), or should it always mean first pick?

## B. Ban phase

4. Exact ban count and order in current Mythic+ Ranked (each side)?  
5. Do bans alternate one-by-one, or blocks? Who bans first relative to coin flip?
6. Can the same brawler be banned by both sides / is there a shared ban pool UI quirk we must mirror?
7. In your friend group, who decides bans — dedicated shotcaller only, or open talk?

## C. Pick sequence (confirm)

8. Confirm this sequence is what you play: **A1 → B1 → B2 → A2 → A3 → B3** (A = coin winners). If wrong, write the real sequence.
9. During B1–B2 (or A2–A3), is the timer shared as one window, or two separate pick timers?
10. Can a player lock early inside a double window, or must both picks land together?
11. If someone disconnects mid-pick, what happens (hover, auto-lock, skip)? Anything the tool should warn about?

## D. Seat order (1st / 2nd / 3rd)

## E. Ownership & legality

## F. Loadouts (gadgets / star powers / gears / HC)
